Storing coffee beans in humid Philippine weather

Heat, humidity and air are the three things that make coffee go stale fastest, and the Philippines has plenty of the first two. A few simple habits keep your beans tasting fresh for longer.

Use an airtight, opaque container

Keep beans in their resealable valve bag or move them to an airtight canister that blocks light. Glass jars look nice but let light in.

Keep them cool, dry and away from the stove

A kitchen cabinet away from the stove, oven and sunny windows is best. Avoid the top of the refrigerator, which gets warm.

Skip the fridge

Opening a cold container in a humid kitchen causes condensation, and coffee absorbs smells. If you must freeze beans, freeze them in small sealed portions and do not refreeze.

Buy smaller amounts more often

Buy what you will finish in two to four weeks. Whole beans keep longer than ground, so grind just before brewing if you can.
